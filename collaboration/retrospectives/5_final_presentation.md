# Retrospective

For the final milestone retrospective, we decided to include our lessons learned from the whole CDSP and from creating slides that represent our progress.

## Lessons learned from CDSP

* Sharing individual progress on tasks every week is crucial for ensuring everyone is on the same page when working in a team.

* Weekly meetings, held at least twice a week, are crucial for maintaining communication and tracking progress.

* Team members should support one another, whether by providing technical assistance or helping to finalize and submit work when others are unavailable.

* Tasks are distributed evenly among team members to promote a balanced and fair workload.

* Setting clear milestones and planning are important when working in a team.

* It’s important to always have a backup plan and consider alternative options in case things don’t go as expected.

## Lessons learned from this milestone

* We learned how to create clear and effective slides that tell a story by using a variety of styles and elements, such as diagrams, bullet points, and images.

* Preparing a supportive draft for the slides is also a valuable approach—it allows any team member to present if needed and ensures that all key points are covered during the presentation.
