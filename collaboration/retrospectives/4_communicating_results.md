# Milestone Retrospective

During this milestone we prepared a Communication Strategy document outlining our target audience (university administrations), their capabilities and constraints, and our strategy for reaching them.

We developed a communication artifact in the form of formal email sent to universities, asking for Moodle logs showing student interactions, engagement, performance, and attendance.

We believe this was the most effective method, as it allowed us to directly engage the institutions and obtain accurate, authoritative information.

The outcome of our communication was the creation and sending of this email, which will lead to obtaining the requested data for continuing our research.

All milestone deliverables, including the survey and labeled Git tag, were completed on time.

Overall, our group worked effectively, and maintaining good communication throughout the process via meetings and communication in Slack.
