# Communication Strategy

## 1. Target Audience

Our primary audience consists of **university administrations** and **faculty members** located in Gaza and neighboring regions affected by the conflict.

They mainly represent universities with direct access to **accurate and detailed student-level data**.

---

## 2. Capabilities and Constraints

### Capabilities

- Possess first-hand institutional data about student enrollment and academic performance, including students' activity on **Moodle**.
- Have administrative staff who can compile reports and respond to research inquiries.
- Understand the local education environment and its current challenges.

### Constraints

- Security risks and operational disruptions due to the ongoing war.
- Harsh living conditions due to the war.
- Institutional and governmental rules regarding the sharing of sensitive or personal data.
- Limited communication infrastructure in certain areas (internet and electricity outages).

---

## 3. How We Intend to Reach Them

**Strategy:** Direct formal outreach through **personalized emails**.

- **Format:** Professionally written, concise email explaining:
  - Research purpose.
  - Potential impact.
  - Findings so far.
  - Specific data requested.
- **Channel:** Email to universities located in Gaza and potentially outside Gaza as well.

---

## 4. What We Would Like Them to Learn

From our communication, the universities would learn that:

- We are conducting an **evidence-based study** on the impact of the Gaza war on students’ academic performance, activity, and attendance.
- Our current findings are based on **indirect datasets** (casualties, displacement, infrastructure damage, and food supply disruptions), but we need **primary educational data** to make accurate conclusions.
- Their contribution of data is **crucial** for a clearer global understanding of the war’s impact on students and institutions.
- The data will be handled **ethically**, with full respect for confidentiality and security.

---

## 5. How We Hope They Will Act

We hope that universities will:

1. Express willingness to collaborate.
2. Provide access to **aggregated or anonymized data** relevant to our research.
